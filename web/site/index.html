Machine learning without std.

Four small Burn programs, from inference to on-device training to a pretrained language model. The same #![no_std] Rust runs here as WebAssembly with zero imports, on the desktop and on an emulated Cortex-M33 with the 520 KiB of RAM of a Raspberry Pi Pico 2.

1InferenceA 321-parameter model approximates sin(x). Watch it learn. 2Training on the deviceLearns your gestures from a phone’s motion sensor. 3Real signalsLearns four spoken words from five recordings each. 4A pretrained LLMTinyStories 260K writes stories, weights used in place.
